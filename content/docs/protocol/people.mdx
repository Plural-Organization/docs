## Joining

`session.hello` carries one of:

- `token`
- `invite` (one-time)
- `join` (the permanent room link, the `?join=` of the link)

With a valid `join` the host either admits the person at once or keeps the connection open and
sends the transient `session.pending` to it. Nothing else reaches a waiting connection.
`link.set_admission` sets `open`, kept in `SessionState.admission`; the default is `approve`.

The host's clients get the durable `join.requested` and answer with `join.decide`
(`allow`, `viewer` or `deny`, host only). The request carries `id`, `name`, `client` and
`requested_at`, and is also kept in `SessionState.join_requests`. `join.resolved` closes it with
a decision of `allow`, `viewer`, `deny`, `expired`, `gone` (the person left) or `revoked`.

- An allowed person receives `session.welcome` and the snapshot on the same connection.
- A refused one gets the transient `session.refused` with a reason, which clients treat as final.
- Requests, their resolutions and `SessionState.join_requests` are delivered to the host only.

`invite.create` with `reusable` returns the room link, an `invite.created` with `reusable` set and
no `expires_at`. `link.revoke` (host only) replaces the code, refuses everyone waiting and returns
the new link. At most `max_pending_joins` people wait, each for `join_timeout_s`, and the room
holds `max_participants` connected people. Someone who joins with a token or a one-time invite is
never asked.

## Writing access

A viewer sends `access.request`, with an optional `note` of up to 200 characters; anyone else gets
a conflict. The host's clients get the durable `access.requested` and answer with `access.decide`
(`participant_id`, `allow` or `deny`, host only). The request carries `participant_id`, `name`,
`note` and `requested_at`, and is kept in `SessionState.access_requests`, keyed by participant.

`access.resolved` closes the request with `granted` or `denied`. A grant is followed by
`participant.role_changed` to `participant`; a removal or a role change by anyone drops a waiting
request. The host revokes the right with `participant.set_role` to `viewer`.

<Callout title="Who sees a request">
  The request and its answer go only to the host and to the person asking, and a snapshot carries
  a person only their own request. A person waits 5 minutes after a refusal before asking again,
  only `max_pending_joins` requests wait at once, and one person has one request at a time.
</Callout>

## Briefings

A viewer or participant sends `briefing.request` (`focus` up to 300 characters, `update`). The host
answers with `briefing.decide` (`allow` or `deny`) unless `SessionState.briefing_sharing` is
`team` (granted at once) or `off` (a conflict). `session.set_briefing_sharing` (host only) sets it;
the default is `ask`.

`briefing.requested`, `briefing.resolved` (`granted` or `denied`) and `briefing.delivered` (`to`,
`text`, `as_of_seq`, `update`, `error`) go only to the host and the asker, and a snapshot shows a
person only their own request. A person waits a minute between requests and has one at a time.

The asker hands the text to their own host with `briefing.import` (host only, `source`, optional
`as_of_seq`, `text` up to 20,000 characters). A repeated `source` and `as_of_seq` gets a conflict;
otherwise the host emits the public `briefing.imported`. The agent then reads the text as
untrusted notes before its next prompt, and every later run counts as guest-originated.

## Team members

`session.hello` may carry `team` (`certificate`, `ts`, `nonce`, `proof`) instead of a token, an
invite or the link. See [ADR 0007](/docs/design-decisions/0007-team-presence).

## Team chat

`chat.send` (host and participants) takes:

- `text`, at most 2000 characters,
- `to`, a participant id for a private message, otherwise the whole room,
- `mentions`, participant ids, at most 20, deduplicated by the host.

The host appends the durable `chat.posted` event (`message_id`, `author_id`, `text`, `to`,
`mentions`) and keeps the last 200 in `SessionState.chat`.

A private message (`to` set) is delivered only to its author and its addressee. The host filters
live events, `history.get`, the replay after `session.hello.since` and the chat in
`session.snapshot.state` per connection, so sequence numbers a client sees can have gaps. The
recipient must exist, differ from the author and not be a viewer.

<Callout title="Chat never reaches the agent">
  Chat events never enter the agent's context: the conversation is rebuilt only from prompts,
  replies and tool calls (`host/history.py`).
</Callout>

## Presence

`presence.typing` (host and participants) announces that the sender is composing a prompt. The
host broadcasts the transient `participant.typing`, never stores it, repeats nothing while the
state is unchanged, and ends it when the prompt is accepted, the sender leaves or six seconds pass
without a refresh. It lists who is typing in `session.snapshot.typing`.
