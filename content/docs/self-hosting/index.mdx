A room has two parts, and each can live anywhere:

- The **relay** routes frames between the host and the people in the room. It never decides
  permissions and never stores content. Run one per team, or share one.
- The **host** runs the agent with your key and keeps the log. On a server it connects *out* to
  the relay, so it needs no public address and no open port.

People join with an invite from the terminal (`plural join "wss://…"`) or from the web client. A
web client served over https can only reach a relay over `wss://`, which is why the relay sits
behind TLS.

<Callout title="Just trying it?">
  You do not need any of this. `plural` already works alone, on your network and over the
  internet through the Plural relay. See [Share a session](/docs/usage/sharing) first.
</Callout>

## Which setup fits

| You want | Run |
|---|---|
| to try it alone | `plural` |
| to show a session to someone on your network | `F2` → Session → *Same network*, then `plural` |
| to show a session to someone anywhere, right now | `plural --tunnel cloudflared` |
| a link that stays the same for your team | a [relay](/docs/self-hosting/relay) on Railway, Render or your own server, then `plural --relay wss://…` |
| an agent that keeps running when your laptop is closed | [the host in the cloud](/docs/self-hosting/cloud-host) |
| a host on a home server or a Mac mini | [your own machine](/docs/self-hosting/own-machine) |

## Options for scripts and servers

The choices you make in the client exist as options too: `--relay <url>` for the internet and
`--bind 0.0.0.0 --public-url ws://<your address>:<port>` for the network. Every option can also be
an environment variable, and a flag wins over a variable. An address given as a flag also wins
over the saved choice in the client.

| Option | Variable |
|---|---|
| `--relay` | `PLURAL_RELAY_URL` |
| `--bind` | `PLURAL_BIND_HOST` |
| `--public-url` | `PLURAL_PUBLIC_URL` |
| `--port` | `PLURAL_PORT` |

## When something drops

If the relay restarts or the network drops, nothing needs to be done. The host reconnects on its
own within a few seconds and takes its room back. Guests see *connection lost, reconnecting…*,
keep their seat and carry on. If a tunnel stops, the host shows a warning in the conversation,
because the links through it no longer work.

<Callout type="warn" title="Trust the relay you pick">
  A relay sees who connects and when, and the size of every message. Use one you run yourself or
  one you trust. Guests use the room link and wait for you to let them in, see
  [Let people in](/docs/usage/sharing#let-people-in).
</Callout>
