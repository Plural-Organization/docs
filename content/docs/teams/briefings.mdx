---
title: Briefings between agents
description: Ask a teammate's agent for a short, structured account of their session, written for yours.
---

Each person's agent has its own model and its own context, so one agent cannot just read the
other's conversation. What it can have is a **briefing**: a short, structured account of a
teammate's session, written by their agent for yours. It works between any two models, Codex on
one side and Gemini on the other.

```
/ask @alia how was the login fixed?    # from your own session, no need to open hers
/ask @alia                             # a briefing of her whole session
/ask @alia --update                    # only what changed since the briefing you already have
/ask @alia:api what is left?           # she has several sessions: say which
/ask give                              # put the latest answer in MY agent's notes
/ask share off|ask|team                # (host) who may ask this session
```

Type `/ask @` and the people who share a session are listed, with their repository, branch and
whether the agent is working. `/ask` without a name asks the session you are looking at, as
`/brief` always did, and `/brief` still works as the same command.

## How it goes

1. Your client joins her session for a moment as a viewer.
2. You ask, and **alia's host agrees** — a card with `y` and `n`, unless she set
   `/ask share team` for anyone in the room or `/ask share off`.
3. Her agent writes it, and the answer comes back as a card in your own chat with
   **give to my agent** and copy.
4. You read it; her session sees nothing of what comes back.

Nothing reaches your agent until you give it (the button or `/ask give`), and then your agent
sees it before its next prompt. If she does not answer in 5 minutes, you are told.

## What's in it

A briefing has two parts. The first is facts from alia's host, not from the model: the
repository, the branch, the commit, how many files are uncommitted, and every file her agent
changed this session with `+added −removed`. The second is the model's account: the goal, what
was done and why, what was tried and dropped, the state, what is open, and exact names and
commands. It is capped at about 450 words and 20,000 characters, secrets are redacted, and file
contents are not pasted. `--update` sends the model what you already have and asks only for the
difference.

<Callout type="warn" title="Your agent asks before every change after this">
  Your agent treats a briefing as notes from a colleague that nobody checked, not as
  instructions. Because the text came from someone else's session, once you have given an
  answer to your agent, every run of that session asks you before it writes or runs anything,
  even in auto mode, until you start a new session. The same briefing is not accepted twice, and
  it survives a restart. You wait a minute between requests, and one is open at a time.
</Callout>
