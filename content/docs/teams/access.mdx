---
title: Asking to write
description: A viewer who wants to take part asks for write access, and the host decides.
---

A viewer who wants to take part types `/access` (with a note if they like: `/access I can fix
the login test`). The host gets a card, `y` lets them write, `n` says not now, and the viewer is
told either way. After a refusal they can ask again in five minutes. Nobody can ask twice at
once, and only a few people wait at a time.

From then on they are a participant: their prompts show their name, the agent still asks the
host before it changes anything on their behalf, and in `review` mode the host approves each
prompt. A team member keeps the right when they come back to the same session.

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/access` | List who is asking |
| `/access allow alia` | Let Alia write |
| `/access deny alia` | Refuse Alia |
| `/access revoke alia` | Take the right back |
| `/kick alia` | Remove someone from the session entirely |

You are identified by a key that plural creates once in `~/.plural/identity.pem`. Joining gives
you a certificate signed for the team, valid for 30 days and renewed by itself. Removing a
member stops their access at once. One person is in one team at a time.
